Worst Alarm Clock Ever

U1.2 / PCB

Golden Arduino Board - PCB Design

A 2-layer Arduino Uno I designed in Altium and assembled, using the same ATmega328P, CH340G USB to UART chip, and header layout as a commercial board so any shield fits. The layout is built to reduce switching noise, with a continuous ground plane, no copper pour on the signal layer, a decoupling capacitor at every VCC pin, and a second row of ground pins beside the digital I/O. It is the most complex board I have designed and assembled so far, and it worked without any debugging.

I measured it against a commercial Uno with a switching noise shield. Near-field emissions dropped to 7% of the commercial board (173 mV to 12 mV, against a target under 10%), and quiet-high droop dropped to about 30% (about 280 mV to 80 mV). Quiet-low ground bounce improved only 8 to 18% and slammer noise stayed about the same, since those are set mostly by the package ground inductance and the shield itself.

U1.3 / Daisy Seed3

ShapeShifter Hybrid Synthesizer

A portable hybrid synthesizer my 6-person senior capstone team is building for an industry client, on a Daisy Seed3 (STM32H750). As project manager and software manager, I built the full breadboard prototype, wiring 32 panel controls into a C++ audio engine with oscillators and a granular sampler. The firmware splits a real-time audio callback from a control loop that reads the controls through 12-bit SPI ADCs and drives preset-recall LEDs over I2C.

U1.5 / STM32

Wake-Word Detection on STM32

A real-time keyword spotter in C++ on an STM32F407. A 4-class CNN runs through ExecuTorch on 16 kHz PDM microphone audio, using STFT features and a 1 MB flash budget. Trained with time-shift, gain, and noise augmentation, paired with a tuned voice-activity detector, and validated in Renode simulation and on hardware.

Board-02: Layout Noise Comparison

Two identical 74AHC14 hex inverter circuits driven by an LMC555 on one board, one laid out with a ground plane and a close decoupling capacitor and one without, to compare switching noise. It worked on first power-up, and scope measurements showed the ground plane cut quiet-low ground bounce from 900 mV to 500 mV.
